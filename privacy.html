Privacy Policy

Society Hub · last updated 16 November 2026

Society Hub is a free app that a housing society's committee uses to manage maintenance payments, notices and complaints. This page explains what information the app keeps and how it is used.

What we keep

  • Your name, email address and villa number, added by your society's committee.
  • When you sign in with Google: only your name and email address. We never see your Google password.
  • Maintenance bills, and the UPI reference number (UTR) you enter after paying.
  • Complaints you raise and notices the committee posts.

Who can see it

  • You see your own bills, your own complaints and your society's notices.
  • Your society's committee admins see all residents, bills and complaints of that society, so they can manage it.
  • Other residents cannot see your bills, email or phone number.
  • Visitors to the public home page see only the society name, address, amenities and notices the committee marks as public.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not show ads or track you across other websites.
  • We do not handle your money. You pay your society directly through your own UPI app.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India, protected by access rules so each person sees only what they are allowed to. Your society's committee may also keep a backup copy in their own Google Sheet.

Removing your data

Ask your society's committee to remove you. They can delete your account from the Members page. For anything else, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
